# LT9-LA2-P1L3 Marker Identity Attribution

Date: 2026-09-23

## Purpose and boundary

P1L3 is a HotpotQA discovery replay. It tests whether repeated occurrences of
one marker identity can inflate an occurrence-count plurality into a false
phenotype route. It does not change the qualified dispatcher, pending-credit
semantics, learning state, retrieval, or serving. HotpotQA remains discovery
evidence and is not eligible to qualify a revised router.

P1L2's frozen episode set and shared-document caveat remain unchanged. P1L3
replays the complete LA2-B adjacent-event episode stream, so consecutive
episodes may share an event/document; all episode counts are descriptive, not
independent-sample counts.

## Frozen input contract

- Corpus: existing HotpotQA `corpus.jsonl`, checked against its previously
  recorded SHA-256.
- Candidate relation bank, marker-family lexicons, tokenization, nearest pair,
  8-token context padding, title/body concatenation, candidate episode
  formation, tie resolver, credit router, decay, semantic deadline, and pending
  capacity are copied from the LA2-B contract.
- No qrels or external relevance/validity labels are read.
- The candidate relation bank's expected context is used only after routing,
  to report the same post-hoc validity and invalid-authority outcomes used by
  LA2-B.

## Marker identity attribution

For every observed event endpoint and each of finance, geography, and
transport, record:

- total raw marker-token occurrences;
- the distinct active marker identity bitmask and its population count;
- repeated occurrence surplus, `R_f = raw_occurrences - distinct_identity_count`;
- per-identity occurrence counts and their before / between / after location
  counts relative to the source-target pair;
- identities contributing repeated surplus to a raw winning family.

Raw occurrence plurality remains the baseline. The single discovery
counterfactual changes only the vote count to the number of distinct active
marker identities. `unique_endpoint_agreement` and every credit-routing rule
remain unchanged.

## P1L3 risk signature

An episode has the label-blind repeated-identity signature only when all hold:

1. The raw-count route is `TIE_RESOLVED` under the frozen unique-endpoint
   agreement rule.
2. Its unique raw-count endpoint has a priority inversion: the raw unique
   winner differs from the existing fixed-priority family.
3. The unique endpoint's winning family has repeated surplus, its raw winning
   count exceeds the tied endpoint's count for that family, and both endpoints
   have the exact same distinct-marker identity bitmask for that family. Thus
   repeated occurrences, rather than an additional marker identity, supply the
   winning count gain.

This structural definition uses no validity outcome. The signature is counted
both at endpoint and candidate-episode level; overlapping adjacent-event
episodes are retained as generated by the frozen integration contract.

## External corpus screen and coverage floor

Screen remaining label-uninspected corpus files in the already frozen order:
ArguAna, NFCorpus, Quora, SciDocs, TREC-COVID, Webis-Touche2020. Read only each
corpus JSONL; do not open qrels, judgments, or prior validity labels. NQ and
HotpotQA are closed discovery corpora and excluded from qualification.

Before the structural preflight runs, freeze this minimum risk-state coverage
floor for a future qualification corpus:

- at least 8 P1L3 risk-signature episodes;
- signatures span at least 2 candidate relations.

Eight is a conservative coverage floor below the 20 repeated-count/no-new-bit
invalid episodes observed in P1L2's discovery set; the second condition avoids
qualifying on one repeated relation template. Meeting this label-blind floor
only means the risky state is exercised. It does not qualify the policy or
permit labels to be opened.

## Required receipts

The HotpotQA receipt must report baseline parity against the frozen LA2-B
replay, all event endpoint attribution, every episode's raw and distinct-vote
routes, route changes and new abstentions, labelled-candidate post-hoc valid /
invalid transitions, credit replay integrity counters, invalid authority
compartments prevented / introduced, and per-finance-marker occurrence and
repeated-surplus concentration.

The corpus preflight receipt must report corpus hashes, document / event /
episode counts, risk-signature counts and candidate relation coverage, the
frozen floor and input order, and an explicit
`qrels_or_validity_labels_opened: false` field.

## Fresh-corpus expansion (frozen before screening)

The six remaining already-local candidate corpora all failed the structural
coverage floor: ArguAna 0 signatures, NFCorpus 0, Quora 0, SciDocs 0,
TREC-COVID 0, and Webis-Touche2020 2 signatures across 1 relation. Therefore,
before screening any newly acquired corpus, freeze this BEIR candidate order:

1. DBPedia-Entity corpus.
2. CQADupStack, with its twelve corpus domains kept separate in their published
   alphabetical order (`android`, `english`, `gaming`, `gis`, `mathematica`,
   `physics`, `programmers`, `stats`, `tex`, `unix`, `webmasters`, `wordpress`).

These candidates were selected to add different corpus forms after the
Wikipedia-derived HotpotQA discovery corpus: entity descriptions, then
community question-answer text. Preflight uses only the relevant corpus JSONL
members; archive members containing queries or qrels are not extracted or
opened. If a corpus does not meet the frozen risk-state floor, it stays
underpowered and the next candidate in this order is screened. Do not reorder
based on risk counts.

No external qualification, router promotion, LA2-B integration rerun, or
retrieval evaluation is authorized by P1L3. A later external test must use the
first preregistered corpus meeting this state-space floor, freeze the exact
selected policy before opening outcomes, and require zero invalid authority
compartments in addition to the existing safety gates.
