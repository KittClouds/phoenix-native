# P1O2 blind review rubric

For each packet, compare the two natural contexts for the displayed candidate word pair. Each excerpt marks one focal occurrence with brackets. Judge whether the two occurrences express a compatible contextual use of the candidate relation.

- `SAME`: compatible contextual uses of the displayed relation.
- `DIFFERENT`: clearly incompatible uses relevant to the relation.
- `UNKNOWN`: the local text is insufficient or genuinely ambiguous.

Judge the contexts, not their source or shared vocabulary. Shared words alone do not establish `SAME`. Assign exactly one label. Edit only `judgment`; do not alter packet IDs or context text.

The candidate relation is shown to clarify what is being compared. Do not infer a label from any prior experiment or expected category.
