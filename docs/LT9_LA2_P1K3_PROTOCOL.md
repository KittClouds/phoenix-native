# LT9-LA2-P1K3: NQ tie anatomy

Status: discovery-only diagnostic. NQ is not a qualification corpus for this
policy.

P1K3 inspects the ten actionable plurality-tie rows in the frozen NQ P1K2
receipt, covering nine document-pair episodes. It reports existing endpoint
counts, marker masks, side evidence, concentration, and before/after
asymmetry. These views are descriptive and do not update lexical authority or
serving behavior.

The only policy candidate carried forward is categorical unique-endpoint
agreement:

1. plurality remains the primary assignment;
2. if one endpoint has a unique marker-count winner and the other endpoint has
   a two-way tie containing that family, route to the unique family;
3. all other ties abstain.

There is no numeric threshold search, weighted score, retrieval tuning, or
NQ requalification. The candidate is selected from the frozen NQ anatomy and
must be evaluated unchanged on the next unopened eligible corpus, HotpotQA.

The statistical unit for qualification is a document-pair episode. Directional
rows remain in receipts for compatibility, but two directions from the same
document pair do not count as two independent routing failures.
