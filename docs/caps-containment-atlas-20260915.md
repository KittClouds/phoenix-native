# CAPS containment atlas qualification

CAPS generation 13 is a containment atlas. Radius encodes text granularity:
document, chapter, paragraph, then sentence from the center outward. Angular
regions encode explicit parent containment; a child keeps its parent's angular
address while occupying a finer shell. Canonical/context roots use a separate
hemisphere and do not consume document territory or acquire invented ownership.

The shell rail exposes four named levels. `Inspect space` removes topology,
nodes, labels, and picking without mutating the publication. Selecting a level
peels all finer shells and refits the camera. `Open cutaway` removes a stable
world-space wedge so inner shells can be read while orbiting. `Show graph`
restores the full scene.

Shortrun live acceptance published generation 13 with 6,941 nodes and 9,736
edges. Compared with generation 12, identities, topology, node styles, and edge
styles are byte-identical in all six manifolds. CAPS is the only changed
position page. The live census is 1 document at radius 4.7965, 10 chapters at
12.7926..12.8134, 1,014 paragraphs at 24.7840..24.8159, and 2,361 sentences at
36.7840..36.8160. The guide page has 36 shell strokes and 96 major containment
boundaries; every coordinate is finite.

Focused validation passed 35 scene-contract tests, 24 compiler tests, 9
publisher tests, and 57 renderer tests. The 25,000-node release benchmark
measured CAPS median 6.306 ms and p95 6.606 ms. This is display-chart geometry,
not a calibrated hyperbolic metric or semantic-similarity embedding.
