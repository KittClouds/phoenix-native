# P1P3 contextual-compatibility review

For each packet, compare the two natural contexts for the displayed candidate word pair. Each excerpt marks one focal occurrence with brackets. Decide whether the two occurrences provide compatible local context for the same use of the displayed lexical relation.

- `SAME`: the two occurrences support compatible uses of the relation.
- `DIFFERENT`: the contexts support clearly incompatible uses of the relation.
- `UNKNOWN`: the excerpts do not provide enough local evidence, or the comparison is genuinely ambiguous.

Judge each pair independently. Do not infer a label from shared words alone, from the candidate pair alone, or from what you think another packet in a group should be. Do not force consistency across three-context combinations; pairwise judgments need not form equivalence classes.

The candidate words are shown only to identify the relation being compared. Do not infer a label from any prior experiment or expected category. Assign exactly one label. Edit only the `judgment` value in the separate judgments template; leave packet IDs and packet text unchanged. Do not discuss labels with the other reviewers until all review files are sealed.
